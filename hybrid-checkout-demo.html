Hybrid checkout demo

Find booking flow activities through the Galaxy API, book one in the Citybreak Online3 booking flow widget, read the visitor's basket id from Online3, then add the customer and commit the basket through Galaxy. Each call appears on the right as it runs. Everything runs in your browser, and what you enter is only sent to Galaxy and Online3.

Use Chrome or Edge, since the widget's Online3 session cookie is a third-party cookie on this page. Committing creates a real booking in the environment you pick, so cancel it afterwards.
